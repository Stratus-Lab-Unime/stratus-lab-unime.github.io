<div class="faq-item" id="topics">
  <h2>What is the course about?</h2>
  <p>Designing and building embedded and cyber-physical systems: programming a microcontroller, acquiring data from analog and digital sensors, and driving actuators. The course works on the Arduino UNO board and its ATmega microcontroller, from digital and analog I/O to serial communication, shift registers and motor control, and asks you to take real-time requirements, cost and power consumption into account when designing a system of your own.</p>
